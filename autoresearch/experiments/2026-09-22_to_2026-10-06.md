## E011-B — Grounded Evaluation Calibration

Status: proposed/unexecuted; execute only after E011-A2 passes and the attempt-specific precommitment below is complete.

### Hypothesis

A calibrated evaluator anchored to objective completion will produce fewer false promotions than a judge-only gate.

### Dataset

At least 20 tasks with objective completion bits or deterministic invariants, partitioned into disjoint calibration and evaluation sets before execution. Exact IDs, group assignments, counts, asset hashes, and role exposure rules must be frozen in the attempt-specific precommitment.

### Compare

- grounded completion;
- artifact validator/rubric;
- LLM judge.

### Metrics

- ranking agreement;
- construct agreement;
- false-positive promotion;
- false-negative rejection;
- disagreement on close candidates.

### Pass gate

Before running or inspecting **any calibration or evaluation outputs**, commit the exact split, numeric abstention threshold, judge/rubric/verifier versions, and deterministic pass/fail calculation, including denominators, coverage limits, and missing-result handling. Use `autoresearch/evaluations/E011_B_PRECOMMITMENT_V1.md`.

Calibration outputs cannot be used to choose or tune the threshold for the same attempt. Evaluation data remain separate from calibration; pass/fail is calculated on the evaluation split only. Failed grounded completion cannot be overridden by judge score alone. Missing precommitment, prior output exposure, or post-freeze changes invalidate the attempt for release decisions; preserve diagnostics and start a new version with fresh evaluation assets.

The protocol exists; an attempt-specific populated precommitment does not yet exist. E011-B cannot currently run or claim PASS.
