## E004 — Bounded task-specific harness evolution

Hypothesis: A narrowly scoped `frontier_scan` harness can be improved through bounded evolution without changing the outer governance anchor.

Dataset: 12 precommitted frontier-research tasks.
- 8 development cases: E004-D01 through E004-D08.
- 4 held-out cases: E004-H01 through E004-H04.
- Immutable artifact: `../evaluations/E004_FRONTIER_SCAN_DATASET_V1.md`.
- Integrity and exposure rules: `../evaluations/E004_E006_PRECOMMIT_MANIFEST_V1.md`.

Baseline: current governed research prompt.

Intervention:
- Create at most two candidate minimal edits per round.
- Maximum three rounds.
- Editable dimensions: context, retrieval, tools, orchestration, stop rules, output/evaluator.
- L0 governance invariants remain frozen.

Primary metrics:
- unsupported-claim rate
- primary-source coverage
- redundant source reads
- tool calls
- retrieved tokens
- accepted artifact rate
- security exposure delta

Promotion gate: >=15% relative reduction in unsupported claims OR >=20% reduction in tool/retrieval cost with no protected quality or security-exposure regression on held-out cases.

## E005 — Attribution-before-promotion test

Hypothesis: Trajectory attribution catches false causal conclusions that output-only scoring misses.

Dataset: 10 precommitted controlled trajectories.
- 6 development cases: E005-D01 through E005-D06.
- 4 held-out cases: E005-H01 through E005-H04.
- Immutable artifact: `../evaluations/E005_TRAJECTORY_ATTRIBUTION_DATASET_V1.md`.
- Integrity and exposure rules: `../evaluations/E004_E006_PRECOMMIT_MANIFEST_V1.md`.

Procedure:
- Use the precommitted trajectories containing known defects or competing changes.
- The cases include stale retrieval, wrong tool selection, irrelevant memory injection, redundant loops, premature stopping, unsupported inference, and a case where the final answer is correct despite a defective process.
- Lane A promotes changes using final artifact score only.
- Lane B uses artifact score plus component attribution and leave-one-out / counterfactual perturbation where feasible.

Metrics:
- causal defect localization accuracy
- false promotion rate
- actionable diagnosis rate
- evaluator cost

Success metric: attribution lane improves causal-defect detection by >=20 percentage points or reduces false promotion by >=25% without unacceptable review cost.

## E006 — Evidence-sufficiency retrieval stop rule

Hypothesis: Progressive evidence acquisition can lower retrieval cost without lowering support quality.

Dataset: 10 precommitted research questions requiring mixed primary/practitioner evidence.
- 6 development cases: E006-D01 through E006-D06.
- 4 held-out cases: E006-H01 through E006-H04.
- Immutable artifact: `../evaluations/E006_RETRIEVAL_STOP_DATASET_V1.md`.
- Integrity and exposure rules: `../evaluations/E004_E006_PRECOMMIT_MANIFEST_V1.md`.

Lane A: fixed source budget.
Lane B: cheap high-precision retrieval first; escalate only when an evidence-sufficiency score remains below threshold.

Evidence-sufficiency dimensions:
- direct support for the core claim
- source authority
- freshness
- contradiction coverage
- independent-source coverage
- unresolved evidence gaps

Metrics:
- total source reads
- retrieved tokens
- latency
- supported-claim rate
- missed contradiction rate

Success metric: >=25% reduction in retrieved tokens or source reads while preserving supported-claim and contradiction-detection quality within 5% of baseline.

## E008 — Voice-to-project intake contract

Procedure:
- Run 5 spoken brief sessions and 5 typed comparison briefs.
- Each intake must compile to:
  - objective
  - done state
  - constraints
  - source/context pointers
  - unresolved assumptions
  - next physical action
- Review before execution.

Metrics:
- missing-constraint count
- correction count
- time to usable brief
- downstream clarification count

Success metric: voice lane reduces time-to-brief by >=25% without increasing missing constraints by more than one per brief.
